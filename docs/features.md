---

## 3. AI Career Discovery Quiz ⭐ (Unique Feature)

Before analyzing the resume, Pathora asks users:

- What are your favorite subjects?
- What are your strongest skills?
- What activities do you enjoy?
- What type of work do you like?
- What are your Top 3 dream careers?
- Do you want a Job, Startup, Higher Studies, Research, or Government Exam?
- Preferred work style:
  - Remote
  - Hybrid
  - Office

This helps AI understand the student better instead of relying only on the resume.
